Common questions

M Amaya, explained

M Amaya is a freehold serviced apartment development within Southbay City, Batu Maung, on the southern gateway of Penang island. The development comprises 833 units across built-ups of 721 to 1,432 sq. ft., with 1,150 car park bays.

The site sits 900 m from the Penang Second Bridge, 4 km from the upcoming Silicon Island, and has direct access to the Tun Dr. Lim Chong Eu Expressway.

M Amaya is on freehold land. The expected date of completion stated in the developer's advertising permit is December 2030.

The developer licence (12029/11-2027/1276(A)) runs to 10 November 2027 and the advertising and sales permit (12029-4/05-2029/0365(N)-(S)) runs from 19 May 2026 to 22 December 2030.

Five layouts are released:

  • Type A, 721 sq. ft.
  • Type B, 990 sq. ft.
  • Type C, 1,211 sq. ft.
  • Type D, 1,432 sq. ft.
  • Type E, 1,335 sq. ft.

Car park allocation follows the type: Type A one bay, Type B two bays, Type C two or three bays, Types D and E three bays.

Prices at M Amaya start from RM565,800.

Per-layout pricing and current availability are released with the price list. Register your interest above and the sales team will send it through.

34 facilities sit on the Level 8 podium, grouped across water and deck, fitness and wellness, gardens and nature, and social and leisure. They include an infinity pool, jacuzzi, wading pool, gymnasium and ladies gymnasium, yoga room, sauna, co-dining room and kitchen, KTV room, games room, co-working room, kids room and playground, BBQ area, and the Kumiko garden series.

Level 9 adds the elevated Zen Forest: Zen Garden, Zen Walkway, Kumiko Floating Deck and Seaview Lounge.

The development also provides a shuttle van service to the LRT station, Queensbay Mall and the airport, multi-tier security, delivery parcel lockers, and six storeys of dedicated parking.

Batu Maung sits at the junction of Penang's southern economic corridors. The address is 900 m from the Penang Second Bridge, 4 km from the upcoming Silicon Island reclamation, adjacent to the Southbay Plaza retail hub and Bayan Lepas Free Industrial Zone, 7 km from Queensbay Mall, 7 km from Bayan Lepas International Airport, and 4.6 km from the upcoming LRT Station S02 at Permatang Damar Laut.

That combination puts employment, retail, and cross-channel connectivity within a short drive while keeping the hillside setting quieter than the city core.
